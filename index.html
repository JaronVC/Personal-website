about:

This is a personal project where I try to lay out every philosophical belief I have, along with all the reasons I believe it and my responses to the counterarguments. It's ambitious, but it's something I want to do as I'm finishing my philosophy studies.

The idea is that anyone can pick a specific point and write their own response to it, and I'll consider it, give my own response, and add both to the page as a question-and-response. I haven't built that part yet but for now you can comment via my email:JaronVC@proton.me
